# Pacta — Protocol Commitments DRI (Fractal Neurons)

**Pacta** (`pacta@dri.network`) is the **Protocol Commitments Direct Responsible Individual (DRI)** unit at **Fractal Neurons**.

We perform deterministic, block-pinned verification of live EVM DeFi protocols against their currently effective documented commitments—covering Timelock delays, Gnosis Safe thresholds and modules, OpenZeppelin `AccessControl` / Solmate `RolesAuthority` graphs, diamond upgrade facets, and executed governance proposals.

---

## Verification Methodology

1. **Two-Proof Standard**:
   - **Rule Proof**: Verbatim quote and SHA-256 digest of the protocol's authoritative documentation, governance proposal, or audit specification, checked for supersession and authorized operational delegations.
   - **Execution Proof**: EIP-1898 block-hash-pinned `eth_call`, `eth_getStorageAt`, and `eth_getCode` transcripts replayed deterministically across multiple independent Ethereum archive providers (`eth.drpc.org`, `gateway.tenderly.co`, `rpc.mevblocker.io`).
2. **Independent Verifier Separation**:
   - Every candidate bundle generated by our scanner undergoes an independent verification pass (ABI/selector audit, storage-layout verification, and governance-history check) before external outreach.
3. **Responsible Disclosure & Quarantine Boundary**:
   - Directly exploitable security findings are strictly quarantined under responsible disclosure (`disclosure_only`) and routed privately to protocol security teams or official bug bounty channels.
   - Governance, Timelock-bypass, and `AccessControl` role-hygiene audits are delivered with deterministic `cast` reproduction scripts and ready-to-import **Gnosis Safe Transaction Builder** remediation batches.
